Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Text Input Data: When you use the App to improve text, your input text is transmitted to our backend server and processed by third-party AI services (via OpenAI API). This text is processed in real-time to generate improved suggestions.

Usage Data: We collect information about your usage of the App, including:

  • Number of API requests made
  • Subscription status and type
  • Features used within the App
  • Device information (device type, iOS version)

Subscription Information: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple App Store and managed by RevenueCat. We receive subscription status information but do not directly process payment information, which is handled securely by Apple.

Optional Identifiers: We may generate and store an anonymous user ID for analytics purposes to improve our service. This ID is not personally identifiable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide AI-powered text improvement services
  • Process and transmit your text to AI services for improvement
  • Track API usage to enforce free tier limits (5 requests per day) and provide unlimited access to premium subscribers
  • Manage subscriptions and provide customer support
  • Improve our App and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Your text input is processed through third-party AI services (OpenAI) to generate improved text. We do not use your text for training AI models, but we cannot control how third-party AI services handle data sent to them.

3. Data Transmission and Third-Party Processing

When you use the App:

  • Your text is transmitted over the internet to our backend server hosted on Vercel
  • Your text is then sent to OpenAI API for AI processing
  • The AI-processed text is returned through the same chain back to your device
  • We do not store your text input after processing, except temporarily in server logs for debugging purposes

By using the App, you consent to this data transmission and processing. We recommend not entering sensitive personal information, passwords, financial data, or confidential information into the App.

4. Information Sharing

We share information with the following third parties:

Service Providers:

  • RevenueCat: For subscription management and payment processing
  • Apple App Store: For subscription purchases and payment processing
  • OpenAI: For AI text processing (your text is sent to their API)
  • Vercel: For backend hosting services

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • To comply with legal requirements or respond to lawful requests
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to users)

Third-party services have their own privacy policies governing their use of information.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Text Input: Your text input is not permanently stored. Text is processed in real-time and may be temporarily logged on our servers for debugging and error handling purposes. Server logs are typically retained for up to 30 days.

Usage Data: We retain usage statistics (API request counts, subscription status) for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Subscription Data: Subscription information is retained as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal and tax obligations.

You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at privacy@promptext.app. Note that we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Backend servers are secured and regularly updated
  • Access to data is limited to authorized personnel
  • We use secure third-party services that implement industry-standard security practices

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. The App requires 'Full Access' permissions for the keyboard extension to function, which enables network connectivity. Use the App at your own risk regarding data security.

10. Keyboard Extension and Full Access Permissions

The App is a keyboard extension that requires 'Full Access' permissions to function. This permission enables:

  • Network connectivity to send text for AI processing
  • Haptic feedback functionality
  • Access to system keyboard APIs

'Full Access' is an iOS requirement for keyboard extensions that need network access. While this permission technically allows the keyboard to access network connectivity, we only use this for the App's core functionality (AI text improvement). We do not access your clipboard, other apps, or personal data beyond what is necessary for the service. The permission is required by iOS design and cannot be partially granted.
